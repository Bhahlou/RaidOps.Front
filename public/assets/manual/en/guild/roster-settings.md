> Officers only.

The **Roster** tab (in guild settings) configures, per active WoW branch, who can join its roster and who gets Officer access.

![Roster tab: access mode and Officer roles per branch](/assets/manual/en/guild/roster-settings-overview.png)

### Roster access

Two modes:
- **Open to everyone** — any member of your Discord can join this branch's roster.
- **Discord role required** — a **Roster roles** picker appears: members holding any of the checked roles can join.

### Officer roles

Independent of roster access: members holding any of the checked roles get Officer access on this branch (editable roster, settings, audit log). Discord server administrators and the server owner always keep full access regardless of this setting.

Every field saves automatically as soon as you pick a value or toggle a mode — no Save button, and branches save independently of each other.

> Activate a branch first in the **General** tab if you don't see it here yet.
