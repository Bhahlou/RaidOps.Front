> Officer only.

**Composition previews** let you simulate what a raid's composition could look like before real characters even exist on the roster — useful right after activating a new branch, before Battle.net import is available for it.

A preview is a named grid of 1 to 8 groups of 5 — pick whatever count fits the format you're planning for (a 40-man is 8 groups, a 25-man would be 5, and so on). Create as many as you like — a target comp for each format, or several drafts of the same one — and rename, duplicate, or delete them from the list. A role counter (tank/healer/melee/ranged) at the top of the composer tallies every spec currently placed.

### Filling a slot

Drag a spec icon from the class palette below the grid and drop it onto a slot — dropping again on an already-filled slot replaces its placeholder. The small ✕ that appears on hover clears a slot's class/spec without touching its note.

Click a slot to add a free-text **note** — who's expected to play it, a question mark, anything. No restriction on what you type; it's shown in the placeholder's class color once a spec is also set.

The two are independent — a slot can hold just a note, just a spec, both, or neither (an empty slot shows its group.slot coordinate instead of an icon).

### What this isn't

A preview has no date, no lockout, and no real character — it's a planning tool, not a raid event. See [Raids](/manual/guild/raid-builder) for scheduling and running actual raids with your roster.
