The 📣 icon in the header takes you to **What's new** — the history of features and improvements added to RaidOps over time, organized by theme.

<img src="/assets/manual/en/account/whats-new-icon.png" alt="What's new icon with badge in the header" width="72" />

A badge appears on the icon when there's unread content. It counts the categories containing something you haven't seen yet, not individual entries.

Entries are grouped into categories (Authentication, Raids, etc.), themselves grouped under broader eras. A category with unread entries opens automatically; the rest stay collapsed so you're not buried in history.

<img src="/assets/manual/en/account/whats-new-groups-overview.png" alt="What's new page with categories grouped under an era" width="700" />

Each entry carries a label: **Feature** for something brand new, **Improvement** for a refinement to an existing feature, or **Fix** for a bug fix. The first two link to the matching manual guide.

Your read status is tied to your RaidOps account, not your browser — anything you've already read stays marked read if you log in from another device.
