The **Calendar**, under **My account**, lets you declare your absences and lateness — as a one-off or on a recurring basis — across every guild you're in. Handy for letting your officers know your availability before a raid gets scheduled, without having to redeclare the same absence guild by guild.

![Calendar overview](/assets/manual/en/account/calendar-overview.png)

### Choosing a scope

Every declaration is **Global** by default — it applies everywhere you play. When creating one, you can instead scope it to one specific branch (only branches where you have an active roster character are offered) if it only concerns that guild. A declaration's scope can't be changed after it's created — edit dialogs show it as a read-only badge instead of a picker.

Since this page merges declarations from every guild and branch, each entry in the **One-off** and **Recurring** panels shows its scope next to it (**Global**, or "*Guild — Branch*") so overlapping declarations stay unambiguous.

### Declaring a one-off absence or lateness

Click **Declare an absence/lateness**, or directly on a day in the calendar. Choose a single date or a date range, a status (**Absent**, **Partial**, or **Available** to override a recurrence on that specific day), and an optional reason.

![Declaring a one-off absence, with the scope picker at the top](/assets/manual/en/account/calendar-exception-dialog.png)

The **Partial** status adds two optional time bounds — *available from* and *available until*. Use either one, or both: lateness is declared with only the start time, an early departure with only the end time.

A one-off declaration always takes precedence over a recurring pattern active on the same day and scope.

### Adding a recurring pattern

Click **Add a recurring pattern** for something that repeats regularly: an evening blocked out every week, or a shift rotation (e.g. a 5-days-on, 2-days-off cycle). Pick **Weekly** mode to check weekdays directly, or **Advanced** mode to define a cycle of any length with its own start date.

Each day of the cycle is set to **Available**, **Absent**, or **Partial**, with the same optional reason and time bounds as a one-off declaration.

![Adding a recurring pattern, with the same scope picker](/assets/manual/en/account/calendar-pattern-dialog.png)

### Editing or stopping a recurring pattern

Editing or stopping a recurring pattern is **never retroactive**: days that have already passed keep exactly the status they had at the time, even if you change the rule afterward. The change only applies from today onward.

### Editing or deleting a one-off declaration

The **One-off** panel on the right only lists upcoming or ongoing declarations. Once a declaration has fully elapsed, it disappears from this list — there's no day-to-day value in keeping it there — but it stays visible and intact on the calendar grid itself, as the historical record of what was actually declared at the time. It can no longer be edited or deleted at that point.
